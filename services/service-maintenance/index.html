Service & Maintenance

Superior service 24/7/365: emergency repairs and preventative maintenance.

24/7/365 emergency service Our service team responds around the clock, every day of the year.

The systems we build are the systems we keep running. Our trained technicians deliver integrated mechanical and plumbing solutions, and they respond around the clock when something fails.

After installation, our turnkey service and preventative maintenance contracts protect your investment. Proactive care keeps critical mechanical and plumbing systems running reliably for years, and when you need an emergency repair or a system upgrade, we’re ready.

Assets & solutions

Mechanical

Boilers, chillers, hydronics, steam and condensate systems; evaporative coolers to air-cooled chillers; surgery center humidification and dehumidification; data center equipment; environmental chambers; refrigeration of all types; ice machines and walk-in coolers and freezers.

HVAC & hydronic

Hydraulics, snow melt systems, cooling towers and air handlers.

Process systems

Water treatment and process water, reverse osmosis and polishing, sterilization and vacuum-jacketed piping, tempering and water for injection (WFI).

Plumbing

Water heaters and culinary systems, drainage and natural gas, water softeners and fixtures, backflow prevention and filtration.

Medical gas & equipment

Medical gas distribution, cylinder manifolds, vacuum pumps and compressors, system verifications and shutdowns.
